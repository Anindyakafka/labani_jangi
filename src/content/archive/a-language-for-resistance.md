MAP’s event page describes a conversation between Labani Jangi and Soumyadeep Dasgupta on her artistic practice. The programme brought together three series inspired by a poem by Tawfiq Ziad, Naresh Saxena’s *Chambal Ek Nadi*, and the legend of Hijrat.

The event was scheduled for 2 March 2025 at MAP Bengaluru, with conversation in Hindi and Bangla. The linked programme provides the original description and artist profile.
