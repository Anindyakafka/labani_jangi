MAP’s programme for my conversation with Soumyadeep Dasgupta explores my artistic practice through three series inspired by a poem by Tawfiq Ziad, Naresh Saxena’s *Chambal Ek Nadi*, and the legend of Hijrat.

Our conversation was scheduled for 2 March 2025 at MAP Bengaluru, in Hindi and Bangla. You can read the original programme and my artist profile at the source below.
