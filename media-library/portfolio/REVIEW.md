# Portfolio review and suggested website uses

Reviewed the supplied **72-page PDF and 72-slide PowerPoint**: all pages visually, all selectable text, and enlarged views of the artist statement, Bibir Darga catalogue, publication covers and collective statement. The files are preserved under [source](source/). The PowerPoint offers original embedded image files; the PDF offers a stable reading copy. A machine comparison of the supplied PDF with a PowerPoint-generated PDF is recorded in [comparison.json](comparison.json), including any rendering differences. A complete page/text index is in [pages.json](pages.json).

The portfolio is much more useful as the basis for **several project pages and a first-person account of the practice** than as a single large slideshow on the homepage. It provides context that isolated social-media images lack: reporting projects, poetry credits, exhibition narrative, publication work and collective authorship.

Comparison result: all **72 pages have matching normalized text**, and **71 pages render identically at half scale**. Page 22 has a rendering difference; enlarged side-by-side inspection showed the same artwork, quotation, credit and link, with no apparent substantive content difference. Both supplied originals remain preserved. Extracted material comprises 115 embedded image occurrences, including collages and repeated pictures; that is not a count of 115 distinct artworks.

## Accuracy and credit issues before publication

1. **Khandera dates:** page 69 says “Started 14th April 2023 May” and also refers to starting from March 2023. Record neither as a settled founding date until Labani clarifies the intended chronology.
2. **Current biography:** the document's “currently pursuing” and “recently” wording will age. Confirm current research status and use explicit award/workshop dates when known. Do not infer artwork dates from file modification times or screenshot filenames.
3. **Publication details:** the page 2 phrase “500 Milis, 2025” appears inside the Palestine-cover description. The image itself contains a “500 Miles” marker. Treat this as ambiguous source wording, not an established publisher name.
4. **Other authors' words:** the portfolio quotes Jitendra Vasava, Pratishtha Pandya, Joshua Bodhinetra, Sabika Abbas, Gokul G.K., Ambedkar and Farrukh Ahmed. Their text must not become uncredited first-person website copy. The same applies to poetry already embedded in images.
5. **Collective authorship:** JINN, Panjeri and Khandera material needs participant and project credits. The portfolio explicitly argues against absorbing collective labour into individual authorship.
6. **Embedded Bengali text:** page 47 is a photographed catalogue page; page 67 has extraction errors. Raw text exports are research aids, not publication-ready transcriptions or translations.
7. **Image availability:** composite slides and photographed artwork can be useful as documentation, but use standalone scans or original embedded pictures when available. No missing detail has been regenerated or upscaled.
8. **External folders:** Drive/Instagram pointers have been recorded as supplied, not downloaded or checked. Their contents are not part of this completed local-folder review.

## Suggested order for later website work

Start with a short first-person introduction from page 4, a Bibir Darga project page, a small credited PARI selection, and a publication-cover section. Then add the fuller labour/migration and Palestine collections, followed by collective practice and readable zines once the remaining credits and source files are available. Use paired English/Bangla entries from the beginning, with the same source artwork and independently written natural alt text in each language.

The folder also contains campaign photographs, campaign posters and general reference documents. They are catalogued for later use, but none has been published or used to generate new campaign copy in this task.
